I BUILT A podcast FROM A BLANK PAGE[Original IP · Brand storytelling · Hosting]

[Brief]
Give a clinical brand a warmer, more human voice that people choose to spend time with.
[My role]
Proposed the format, wrote the scripts, co-hosted, and ran production and post-production.
[Result]
17 episodes; one passed 500K views.

Clinikally Approved did not exist until I proposed it. A clinical brand needed a warmer voice, so I designed a format that puts a doctor’s expertise next to a curious everyday host asking the questions viewers actually have.

I co-host with Dr. Sarkar and Dr. Tasya, write the scripts, and produced the show from the first episode to the seventeenth.

[1/3]

[01]
The format

Each episode follows a simple spine: a short intro from me, a numbered run of questions, and a clear outro. I play the curious, relatable voice. The doctor plays the expert. The audience gets answers without needing a medical degree.

[02]
Beyond the show

I hosted and moderated the brand’s public webinar, “Uncomplicated: Skincare, Simplified by Dermatologists and AI”, with doctors, a nutritionist and a live demo of the brand’s AI routine builder.

Expert answers, without needing a medical degree.

[Impact]
17[episodes released.]
500K+[views on one episode, “Which Serum to Use?”]

Source: YouTube Studio.

[What I’d do next]

Launch each episode clip-first, with three Shorts out before the full episode, and rotate guest doctors to widen reach without changing the format.
